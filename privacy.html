Privacy Notice

This starter notice is designed for the Ceylon Wellness journey enquiry flow. Before launch, replace the placeholders with the legal identity and contact details of the actual data controller.

What we collect

For a journey enquiry, the site may collect your name, email, travel dates, duration, preferences and any information you voluntarily add to the enquiry. Avoid entering medical records or other sensitive health information into the public form.

Why we use it

To respond to your journey enquiry, prepare an itinerary or quotation, coordinate with confirmed travel/wellness providers, and improve the service where you have given the relevant consent.

Your rights

Depending on the applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal data and to withdraw consent. The final privacy notice should state the legal basis, retention periods, recipients, international transfers and contact route.

Analytics & cookies

Optional analytics is designed to load only after the visitor accepts optional cookies in this starter build. See the Cookie Notice.
