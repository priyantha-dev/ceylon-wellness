Cookie Notice

This starter build uses local browser storage for journey preferences and consent choices. Optional Google Analytics is not loaded until optional analytics consent is accepted and a real GA4 Measurement ID is configured.

Essential storage

The journey builder can remember your current planning choices in your browser. These settings are not presented as a booking and do not create an account.

Optional analytics

When enabled, analytics should be configured with the final privacy and consent setup appropriate to the countries where Ceylon Wellness operates. Do not add a Measurement ID until the business has a confirmed Google Analytics property.
